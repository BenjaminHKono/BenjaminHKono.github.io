See you in 2025

Hello race fans! We're pleased to announce that preparations for the 2025 Wiener Nationals have begun.

We hope to see you this July 4 at Rockaway Beach. We've missed the thunder of dachshund paws, seeing you all, and putting on our favorite FUNraising event.

As always, the Firecracker Wiener Nationals will benefit the Tillamook Animal Shelter. All proceeds from every Firecracker Wiener Nationals go towards supporting their vital work.

If you can't make the race or came a previous year and couldn't give, you can still help the Tillamook Animal Shelter.
